VISLite 能做什么？ What can VISLite do?

VISLite 是一个轻量级的数据可视化前端库。我们的目标是帮助你更快速、简单、高效地开发各种可视化业务产品。 VISLite is a lightweight data visualization frontend library. Our goal is to help you develop various visualization products more quickly, simply, and efficiently.
